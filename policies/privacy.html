Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

What we collect

  • Order information: name, shipping address, email, and phone number — used to fulfill and deliver your order.
  • Payment information: processed securely by our payment providers (e.g. Stripe, PayPal). We never see or store your full card details.
  • Usage data: anonymous analytics and advertising cookies (e.g. Google Analytics, Meta Pixel) that help us improve the store and our ads.

How we use it

  • To process, ship and support your orders.
  • To send order updates, and — only if you opt in — occasional offers you can unsubscribe from at any time.
  • To measure and improve our website and advertising.

What we never do

We never sell your personal data. We share it only with the service providers required to deliver your order (payment processing, shipping, email) — and only what they need.

Your rights

You can request a copy of your data, or ask us to delete it, at any time by emailing support@oxirenew.com. EU/UK customers have additional rights under GDPR, and we honor them.

Cookies

We use essential cookies to run the store and optional analytics/marketing cookies. You can block cookies in your browser settings at any time.
