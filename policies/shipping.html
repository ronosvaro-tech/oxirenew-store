Shipping Policy

Processing time

Orders are processed within 1–3 business days. You'll receive a confirmation email as soon as your order is placed, and a tracking number as soon as it ships.

Shipping time & cost

  • Free tracked shipping on every order, worldwide.
  • Typical delivery time: 7–14 business days.
  • During peak seasons or carrier delays, delivery may take up to 20 business days.

Tracking

Every order ships with full tracking. If your tracking hasn't updated for more than 7 days, contact us at support@oxirenew.com and we'll investigate immediately.

Lost or damaged packages

If your package is lost in transit or arrives damaged, we'll send a free replacement or issue a full refund — your choice.
