Returns & Refunds

90-day money-back guarantee

We stand behind OxiRenew. If you're not satisfied with your results for any reason, contact us within 90 days of delivery and we'll issue a full refund.

How to request a refund

  • Email support@oxirenew.com with your order number.
  • Tell us what didn't work — a photo helps us improve, but isn't required.
  • We'll process your refund within 2 business days of approval. Funds typically appear within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank.

Do I need to ship the product back?

In most cases, no — we don't ask you to pay return shipping for a product you're unhappy with. We'll let you know in our reply if a return is needed.

Damaged or incorrect items

If anything arrives damaged or incorrect, send us a photo within 14 days of delivery and we'll ship a free replacement right away.
